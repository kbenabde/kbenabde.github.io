{{ prof.position }} · Machine Learning & Data Mining

I am an Associate Professor (HDR) at Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1, where I teach at Polytech Lyon, and a researcher at the LIRIS laboratory (CNRS UMR 5205), where I co-head the DM2L team.

My research focuses on semi-supervised learning and statistical data mining: optimization with Lp,q-norms, spectral and knowledge graphs, and topological models (SOM), applied to multimodal and multi-view learning, feature selection and co-selection, anomaly detection and clustering. I received my PhD in Data Mining from Université Paris 13 (2003) and my HDR in Machine Learning from Université Lyon 1 (2014).
